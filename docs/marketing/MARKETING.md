# Marketing Positioning & Assets Plan

## Positioning

A five-second video must instantly read: *these people are moving furniture — the
furniture is alive — something has gone very wrong.* Lean into readable silhouettes
and physical consequences over dialogue.

**Comparables (for discovery, not cloning):** chaotic co-op physics games and
supernatural workplace comedies. Differentiator: **creature-care meets moving
sim** — you *befriend/calm* the hazards you also have to carry.

## Six GIF concepts (clip-first)

1. Two players wrestle the **Clingy Sofa** as it hooks a cushion around a doorframe
   and drags one of them back.
2. The **Hungry Refrigerator** swallows the team's straps mid-carry; everyone
   freezes.
3. A player looks away for one second and the **Shy Wardrobe** has moved three
   meters closer.
4. Lights die; the **Anxious Lamp** strobes; the whole team scatters.
5. A "perfect" delivery — then the **van** doors close by themselves.
6. Tiny **Side Table** teleports behind a player; beat; chaos.

## Social hooks

- "Some furniture is harder to let go."
- "We calmed the sofa. We did not calm ourselves."
- "The wardrobe only moves when you're not looking. We tested this. We regret it."
- "Rate our insurance claim."
- "POV: you're a ghost now, but you still have to help."

## Pricing positioning (preliminary — validate before setting)

Party/co-op physics comedies in this space commonly sit in the low indie price
band with a free demo and frequent co-op discounts. **Do market validation**
(wishlist velocity, comparable titles at launch) before committing a number. This
section is intentionally not a fixed price.
