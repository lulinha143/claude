## Short description (English)

Move furniture that has feelings. HEAVY BAGGAGE is a solo & co-op physics
horror-comedy about a paranormal moving company. Investigate living objects, calm
their supernatural breakdowns, and haul them to the van before the house decides
you're not leaving.

## Long description (English)

**Every object has issues.**

You're an underpaid mover for *Heavy Baggage Paranormal Relocation & Emotional
Logistics*. The job is simple: pack the furniture, load the van, get paid. The
problem: the furniture is alive, emotionally unstable, and supernaturally gifted.

- **The sofa** has abandonment issues and will cling to the doorframe (and you).
- **The refrigerator** gets violent when it's hungry — feed it or lose your gear.
- **The wardrobe** won't move while you're looking. So don't look.
- **The lamp** panics in the dark. Everyone panics when the lamp panics.

Investigate each object's emotional baggage, calm it (or restrain it), and carry
it — with real, physical, two-hands-and-a-prayer weight — through impossible
houses full of hazards. Every mistake is louder, heavier, and funnier than the
last.

**Features**
- **Solo or 1–4 player online co-op.** Play alone with AI assistants or bring
  friends and immediately regret it.
- **Physics-first carrying.** Force-based grabbing means the comedy comes from
  understandable consequences, not fighting the controls. Assist options included.
- **20+ sentient objects**, each a distinct mechanical problem, with emotions,
  traits, and rare hidden personalities.
- **Property Instability.** Anger the house and it starts fighting back — lights
  flicker, layouts shift, and eventually it refuses to let you leave.
- **Funny failure.** Get flattened, folded, or boxed — then keep helping as a
  little ghost until a teammate digs you out.
- **Deterministic seeds & daily challenges** to share exact chaos with friends.
- **Incident reports** you can export and share.
- **Deep accessibility** and full controller support. English & Brazilian
  Portuguese at launch.

Some furniture is harder to let go.

## FAQ

**Can I play solo?** Yes — solo with configurable AI assistants, fully offline.
**Do I need Steam to play?** No. The game runs without Steam; Steam features are
optional and fail gracefully.
**Is there PvP?** No, it's cooperative.
**Scary?** Light horror with a reduced-scare mode and a photosensitivity/flashing
toggle.
**Languages?** English and Brazilian Portuguese at launch; more planned.
