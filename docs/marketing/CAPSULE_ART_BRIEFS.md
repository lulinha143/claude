# Capsule Art Briefs

Style: rounded low-poly, bold silhouettes, painted gradients, warm interior vs.
supernatural accent color, subtle film grain. Furniture wears small expressive
faces. No gore, no photorealism, original art only.

- **Focal:** two stubby movers straining to carry the **Clingy Sofa**, whose
  cushion-arm is hooked around a doorframe, eyes wide.
- **Mascot:** Boxy peeking from a cardboard box in a corner.
- **Mood:** warm living room light, one supernatural teal glow leaking from the
  hallway.
- **Title lockup:** "HEAVY BAGGAGE" bold; subtitle "Paranormal Moving Company".
- Keep faces/silhouettes readable at the smallest size; avoid tiny text.
