# Trailer Storyboard (~60s)

Goal: within 5 seconds the viewer understands *moving furniture + it's alive +
it's going wrong*. Cut to gameplay fast; comedy through physics, not narration.
All footage from real gameplay.

| Time | Shot | Audio |
|------|------|-------|
| 0:00–0:03 | Company van pulls up to an ordinary suburban house. Text: "New contract." | Cozy jingle |
| 0:03–0:07 | Two movers lift a sofa — a cushion-arm shoots out and hooks the doorframe, yanking one back. | Fabric squeak, thud |
| 0:07–0:12 | Quick cuts: fridge swallows straps; wardrobe slides closer when the camera looks away; lamp strobes as lights die. | Rising unease |
| 0:12–0:18 | Title card: **HEAVY BAGGAGE — Paranormal Moving Company.** Tagline fades in: "Every object has issues." | Sting |
| 0:18–0:30 | Co-op montage: 4 players fumble a piano down stairs (it plays dramatically), a mirror spawns a worse duplicate, someone gets boxed and returns as a helper ghost. | Comedic swells |
| 0:30–0:40 | The calm-it loop: emotion HUD, applauding the piano, knocking on the wardrobe, feeding the fridge. Instability meter climbs to "Hostile"; the room shifts. | Tense-to-relief beats |
| 0:40–0:50 | The escape: hauling the last object to the van as the house fights back; doors slam; they make it (barely). Incident report card flashes. | Big finish |
| 0:50–0:57 | Boxy peeks out of his box, closes the lid. Feature bullets appear. | Boxy squeak |
| 0:57–1:00 | Wishlist / demo CTA + logo. Tagline: "Some furniture is harder to let go." | Button chime |

## Cutdowns
- **6s vertical** for shorts: shot at 0:03–0:07 (sofa drag) → title.
- **15s**: sofa drag + fridge eat + title + CTA.
