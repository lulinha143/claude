# Steam Store Asset Checklist

> Sizes verified against the official Steamworks "Store Graphical Assets" page in
> **July 2026**. Valve began accepting **larger** asset sizes in **August 2024**,
> and exact requirements can change — always confirm the current pixel sizes on
> the official page before exporting finals:
> https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets

All capsule art must clearly show the game's name/logo and must not contain
prices, review scores, or misleading marketing badges (see the asset rules page).

## Art direction reminders for this game

- Cozy tree-trunk office, warm amber lighting, rounded friendly animals.
- Feature **Pip** (the squirrel founder) prominently; the six-plus employees can
  fill wider capsules.
- Use the game's palette (walnut brown, cream, moss green, amber, muted red).
- Keep the logo readable at the smallest generated size (120 × 45).

## Tips

- Capture screenshots at 1920 × 1080 from the browser build zoomed to fit, or
  from the desktop app expanded, using **Photo Mode** to hide menus.
- Moment Cards (Collections tab) export clean 640 × 400 PNGs — good for social
  posts, though not a substitute for the required capsule sizes above.
- Keep source files (layered) so you can re-export if Steam changes sizes again.
