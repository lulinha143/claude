# Audio licensing

All audio in _Working My Nuts Off!_ is **generated at runtime** with the Web
Audio API (`src/audio/audio.ts`) — background music, office ambience, and sound
effects are synthesized from oscillators and noise. **No audio files are bundled
or downloaded**, so there is nothing to license and no attribution is required.

- **Music:** a slow, warm procedural progression (sustained pad chords + a soft
  triangle melody) generated by a lookahead scheduler. Original, CC0-equivalent.
- **Ambience:** a soft brown-noise "room tone" bed plus randomized, cooldowned
  one-shots (typing, paper, coffee, wood creak, drip).
- **SFX:** short synthesized blips for clicks, collecting, upgrades, events,
  celebration, and prestige.

If real recorded audio is added later, place files under `assets/audio/{music,
ambience,sfx,ui}` and record their source + license here and in
`assets/audio/SOURCES.md`. Use only CC0 / public-domain / permissively-licensed
material cleared for commercial use.
