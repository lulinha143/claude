- **Synthesized audio:** generated at runtime; nothing to license
  (`assets/audio/LICENSES.md`).
- **Office background (`public/backgrounds/office/*`):** supplied by the project
  owner as the game's official visual asset (source: https://imgur.com/a/PFVDy74).
  The project owner is responsible for holding and clearing the rights to this
  asset for distribution (including on Steam). If you fork this project, replace
  it with art you own — see `docs/BACKGROUND_ASSET_PIPELINE.md`.
- **Employee sprites (`public/employees/*`):** supplied by the project owner as
  the game's official character art (e.g. Bea). The project owner is responsible
  for holding and clearing the rights to these assets for distribution
  (including on Steam).

No third-party copyrighted game/movie/cartoon assets are used.
