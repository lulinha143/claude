# Privacy Statement

_Working My Nuts Off!_ is designed to be private by default and works completely
offline.

## What the game stores

- **Your game progress and settings**, saved locally on your device
  (browser `localStorage` in the web version; the app's user-data directory in
  the desktop version). This never leaves your machine unless you manually
  export a save file and share it yourself.

## What the game does NOT do

- It does **not** create an account or require a login.
- It does **not** send your gameplay, settings, or any data to a server. Core
  gameplay makes no network requests.
- It contains **no advertising, no analytics/telemetry, no tracking, no
  in-app purchases, no loot boxes, no cryptocurrency, and no NFTs.**

## Activity detection (Focus Harvest)

Focus Harvest is **optional and disabled by default**. When enabled on the
desktop, the game may check **only how many seconds the computer has been
inactive** (via the operating system's idle timer).

It specifically does **not** record, read, or transmit:

- keystrokes or typed text,
- the contents or titles of any application or window,
- browser history or URLs,
- file names or file contents,
- screenshots of your screen,
- clipboard contents,
- or any other personal information.

You can confirm and toggle this in **Settings → Focus Harvest**, where the same
explanation is shown.

## Moment Cards & Photo Mode

Shareable Moment Cards are rendered entirely from in-game content (illustration,
caption, company name, date, rarity). They never capture a screenshot of your
actual desktop, and no private information is included.

## Steam (if released on Steam)

If distributed via Steam, achievements and stats may be synchronized through
Steam's own services under Valve's privacy policy. This integration is optional
and the game runs fully without Steam.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue or use the security advisory channel
described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
