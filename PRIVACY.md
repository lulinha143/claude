# Privacy Policy (draft — requires professional/legal review)

**HEAVY BAGGAGE** is designed to be privacy-respecting by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent off your device by the core game.** Specifically:

- **No personal information** is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted.
- **No analytics/telemetry** are enabled. The analytics abstraction in the code
  is a no-op interface with no network backend.
- **No microphone recording.** Voice chat is not implemented; if it is added in
  future it will be strictly opt-in and disclosed here.
- **No advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs** are bundled.

## Data stored locally on your device

- **Save games** and **settings** are stored in Godot's `user://` directory
  (per-OS app data). They never leave your machine unless you use Steam Cloud
  (which syncs the same files through Steam, governed by Valve's policies).
- **Incident report PNGs** you choose to export are written locally only. Export
  renders the in-game card, never your desktop.

## Multiplayer

Online co-op uses peer-to-peer / direct connections. Your IP address is visible
to peers you connect to, as with any direct multiplayer connection. No game
server operated by the developer stores your data.

## Steam

When built and distributed via Steam, Steam features (achievements, Cloud saves,
friends invites) are handled by the Steam client and are subject to
[Valve's Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under the age required by your local law
for data processing, and collects no data regardless.
